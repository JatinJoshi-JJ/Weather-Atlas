Icon Meaning and Accessibility Guide

Ensuring meteorological states remain immediately recognizable across visual, cognitive, and assistive domains.

Non-Color-Only Indicators

No weather condition relies solely on color hues. Each symbol incorporates distinctive geometric silhouettes (e.g., zig-zag lightning bolts, stellar snowflake branches, diagonal rain dashes).

Polite Screen Reader Announcements

Condition switches trigger concise announcements via an aria-live="polite" region rather than re-reading the entire page, eliminating cognitive overload for assistive technology users.

Keyboard Focus Ring Visibility

All interactive buttons, chips, native selects, and cards maintain high-contrast :focus-visible indicators with comfortable 44px minimum touch target dimensions.
